# Current Meeting Detail Enrichment

Updated **328** staged meeting drafts from their individual current public detail pages. **3** records did not expose a complete structured street-address and city pair; all others received the public street, suburb, city, notes, online link, phone, and meeting-type detail available on the source site.

The script does not change a meeting’s draft or correction status, does not mark any QA item as complete, and does not publish any meeting. National review remains required before a record can appear in the public finder.
