# Homepage Hero Image Prompt — Nano Banana 2K

## File name

`na-homepage-south-africa-region-hero-2400x1350.webp`

## Generation prompt

Create a wide cinematic editorial documentary photograph for the **Narcotics Anonymous South Africa national homepage**. The image must communicate a hopeful, grounded beginning and a sense of safe human connection without showing drug use, medical drama, identifiable patients, or staged advertising.

**Subject:** A quiet South African coastal civic landscape at first light, viewed from a welcoming pedestrian promenade. Include a recognizable but non-specific South African harbour setting: calm ocean, a long walkway, subtle harbour lights, distant hills or coastline, indigenous coastal vegetation, and one or two empty benches suggesting that a person can arrive, pause, and take a first step. The scene should feel real, local, dignified, and emotionally reassuring rather than touristy.

**Composition:** Very wide horizontal website hero, **16:9 aspect ratio**, target output **2400 × 1350 pixels**, with the main visual interest on the **right half and far background**. Keep the entire **left 40% visually calm and relatively uncluttered** for the website headline and two call-to-action buttons. Use a natural leading line from the lower-left promenade toward the brighter horizon or harbour on the right. Keep the horizon around the upper third. Use enough depth and texture that the image remains recognisable behind a subtle navy-blue accessibility overlay.

**Mood and lighting:** Early morning blue hour transitioning into soft warm sunrise. Hopeful, peaceful, honest, and restorative. Use natural atmospheric light, gentle cloud detail, realistic water reflections, restrained contrast, and documentary colour grading. The image should work with white typography overlaid on the left without losing legibility.

**Brand fit:** The photograph will sit behind a Narcotics Anonymous South Africa homepage hero. It must feel compatible with a deep NA navy/blue interface, white typography, and restrained green action accents. Use natural blues, slate, muted sea green, warm sunrise highlights, and neutral stone tones. Do not add any visible brand logos or invented organisation signage.

**People:** Prefer no people. If people are included, show only one or two distant, anonymous adult silhouettes walking calmly along the promenade; no faces, no identifiable personal details, no clinical clothing, no staged embrace, and no exaggerated emotion.

**Technical requirements:** Photorealistic, high-end editorial documentary photography, true-to-life South African environment, crisp but natural detail, wide dynamic range, realistic perspective, no text, no watermark, no border, no collage, no split-screen, no graphic design, no illustration, no 3D render. Preserve clean negative space on the left for HTML text overlay. Generate at **2K quality** and export as a high-quality WebP or PNG suitable for a responsive web hero.

**Avoid:** Any text or readable signage; logos; watermarks; drug references; needles, pills, substances, rehabilitation clichés, hospital scenes, sad isolated faces, generic stock-photo handshakes, crowds, staged religious imagery, unsafe cliffs, visual clutter in the left text area, extreme lens flare, oversaturated teal-and-orange grading, fantasy architecture, inaccurate African landmarks, distorted benches, duplicate objects, warped horizons, malformed people, or artificial-looking skies.

## Acceptance check

The final image passes when it is a recognisable, calm South African coastal scene; the left 40% remains usable for the homepage headline and CTAs; the photograph remains visible under a moderate navy overlay; there is no generated text or watermark; and the image is delivered at or above 2400 × 1350 pixels in a web-safe format.
