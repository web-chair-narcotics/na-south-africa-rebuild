# Narcotics Anonymous South Africa
## Five High-Definition Website Hero Image Prompts

**Prepared for:** Narcotics Anonymous South Africa

**Purpose:** Generate five distinct, premium, documentary-style hero images for the South Africa Region website and its four area websites. Each image must be supplied as a separate file using the exact filename shown below. The filename is the connection key used to place the asset on the correct site; do not rename files after generation.

## Global production specification

| Requirement | Exact instruction |
|---|---|
| Number of images | 5 separate images; never a contact sheet or collage |
| Output format | WebP, high quality, RGB or sRGB colour profile |
| Final dimensions | 2400 × 1350 pixels |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 |
| Intended use | Full-width responsive website hero, desktop share-preview image, and area-site visual identity |
| Composition | Keep the left third calm and low-detail for headline text; place the visual subject or route of light on the centre-right |
| Mobile safety | The main visual idea must survive a 390–430 px wide crop; do not place the only important detail at the extreme edge |
| Colour direction | Natural documentary colour with NA primary-blue atmosphere, off-white highlights, restrained green, and no green-first colour cast |
| Human depiction | No identifiable faces, no staged therapy, no posed handshake, no implied patient or client, no fabricated testimonial scene |
| Text and branding | No words, signs, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, flags, branded clothing, or invented NA emblems inside the image |
| Recovery sensitivity | No drugs, drug paraphernalia, needles, pills, police imagery, hospital imagery, rehabilitation-centre imagery, or crisis spectacle |
| Venue accuracy | Do not depict a recognisable meeting venue or imply that the image is the location of a specific meeting |
| Lighting | Natural early-morning or late-afternoon light; realistic exposure; no artificial lens flare or fantasy glow |

If the image generator cannot output exactly 2400 × 1350 pixels, generate the largest available 16:9 image and resize it to exactly 2400 × 1350 without stretching. Do not crop away the left text-safe zone during resizing.
