# Meeting QA Register

The register contains **328 staged legacy meetings**. **4** in-person or hybrid records have legacy approximate geocodes and should be prioritised for venue and map-pin confirmation.

Every row begins with the four mandatory review items as pending: **address verified**, **map pin confirmed**, **spelling checked**, and **contact confirmed**. An online-only meeting marks map-pin confirmation as not applicable but still requires its other three checks. The platform preserves all records as drafts until a national reviewer completes the applicable checks and publishes the record.
