# NA South Africa Area-Site Image Prompt Pack

**Prepared for:** the South Africa Region, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Pretoria, and KwaZulu-Natal websites.  
**Purpose:** generate five distinct, high-quality hero images that feel like one national NA platform while giving the regional site and each area an unmistakable sense of place.

## Shared image direction

Each image should be a calm, editorial, documentary-style website hero rather than a tourism advertisement. The emotional register is **welcoming, dignified, grounded, hopeful, and non-judgmental**. The image should communicate that a practical next step and a supportive local community are available without showing drug use, medical crisis, prison imagery, religious symbolism, recovery clichés, or staged testimonial scenes.

Use the current NA South Africa visual language as the shared frame: primary blue `#085C84`, hero blue `#026AB9`, action green `#2F9B3E`, soft off-white `#F8F8F8`, neutral charcoal `#54595F`, natural light, moderate contrast, and generous negative space. These are recovery-support website images, not tourism adverts. Do not include written words, logos, badges, signage, watermarks, artificial UI, or identifiable private information. Avoid recognisable faces; if people are present, show distant anonymous silhouettes or partial figures only. Leave the **left third calm and low-detail** for the page headline and CTA, and keep the primary visual interest in the centre-right. Generate one standalone WebP per named asset at **2400 × 1350 px, 16:9**, with enough edge detail for 390–430 px mobile crops. The image must survive desktop hero cropping, preserve its focal point on the centre-right, and remain readable behind white interface text.

## 1. South Africa Region

> Create a high-end editorial documentary photograph for the hero of the Narcotics Anonymous South Africa Region website. Show a wide, quiet South African landscape at first light where a human-scale paved path or open public-space edge leads gently toward the centre-right: layered Highveld grassland and distant low mountains, subtle evidence of a connected country through a far-off road or small civic structure, and a calm sky with room for headline text. This is a national recovery-support website, not a tourism advertisement. Composition: 16:9, 2400 × 1350 px, horizontal website hero, human-eye or slightly elevated viewpoint, the entire left third intentionally low-detail and text-safe, focal interest and leading route in the centre-right, subject still legible in a 390 px mobile crop. Style: premium editorial documentary photography, natural dawn light, restrained blue-green and eucalyptus shadows with small warm highlights, honest South African atmosphere, dignified hope, no spectacle. Constraints: no text, logos, signage, watermarks, flags, political symbols, identifiable faces, staged recovery scenes, drugs or drug paraphernalia, religious symbols, safari animals, resort architecture, collage, split-screen, artificial lens flare, oversaturated colours, or generic stock-photo composition. Make the scene communicate “there is a next step and it is possible to find it” without showing a specific meeting venue.
