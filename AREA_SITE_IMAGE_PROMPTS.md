# NA South Africa Area-Site Image Prompt Pack

**Prepared for:** the Johannesburg, Cape Town, Pretoria, and KwaZulu-Natal area websites.  
**Purpose:** generate four distinct hero images that feel like one national NA platform while giving each area its own sense of place.

## Shared image direction

Each image should be a calm, editorial, documentary-style website hero rather than a tourism advertisement. The emotional register is **welcoming, dignified, grounded, hopeful, and non-judgmental**. The image should communicate that a practical next step and a supportive local community are available without showing drug use, medical crisis, prison imagery, religious symbolism, recovery clichés, or staged testimonial scenes.

Use the current NA South Africa visual language as the shared frame: deep forest green, soft off-white, muted eucalyptus and sage, restrained blue-green accents, natural light, moderate contrast, and generous negative space. Do not include any written words, logos, badges, signage, watermarks, artificial UI, or identifiable private information in the generated image. Avoid recognisable faces; if people are present, show distant anonymous silhouettes or partial figures only. Leave the **left third calm and low-detail** for the page headline and CTA, and keep the primary visual interest in the centre-right. Generate as a wide website hero at **16:9, minimum 2400 × 1350 px**, with enough edge detail for responsive crops. The result must remain usable behind readable white or dark-green interface text and should not depend on tiny visual details.

## 1. Johannesburg

**File name:** `na-area-johannesburg-hero.webp`  
**Page placement:** Johannesburg area landing page hero and social/share preview crop.  
**Visual job:** establish an urban, connected, forward-moving local fellowship without making the city feel anonymous or corporate.

### Generation prompt

> Create a wide editorial documentary photograph for the hero of a Narcotics Anonymous area website in Johannesburg, South Africa. Show a quiet early-morning urban neighbourhood at the edge of the city: warm Highveld sunrise filtering through mature street trees, layered apartment and community-building silhouettes in the distance, a clean pedestrian path leading gently toward the right side of the frame, and a subtle sense of people being able to meet and move forward together. Composition: 16:9 website hero, camera at human eye level, generous calm low-detail negative space across the entire left third for headline text, focal depth and city texture concentrated in the centre-right, no dominant landmark, no readable signs. Style: refined documentary photography, natural warm light balanced with deep forest-green shadow tones, soft sage and muted blue-green accents, realistic South African atmosphere, quiet optimism, premium but human. Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermarks, no branded clothing, no identifiable faces, no staged handshake, no hospital or rehabilitation imagery, no drugs or drug paraphernalia, no religious symbols, no tourist postcard look, no oversaturated orange sky, no artificial lens flare, no excessive blur. Make the scene feel local, safe, open, and suitable for a recovery-support website.

## Generation and review checklist

Generate one standalone image per named file, not a contact sheet. Before delivery, confirm that the left third remains text-safe at desktop width, the central subject survives a narrow mobile crop, no unintended words or logos appear, faces are not identifiable, the image does not imply a specific meeting venue, and the visual tone remains consistent with a national recovery-support service. Keep the source prompt and generated file name together during upload so the area page can be audited later.
