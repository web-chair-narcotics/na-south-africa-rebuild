# Five-Site Image Research Notes

## Reference sources

The prompt pack uses the live [Cape Town Tourism](https://www.capetown.travel/) reference as a visual cue for Cape Town’s official place identity. Its extracted page describes Cape Town as a modern, cosmopolitan city surrounded by nature and centred around Table Mountain, while highlighting beaches, biodiversity, food and wine. The prompt therefore uses a local coastal/foothill/fynbos direction without making a tourism postcard or relying on a single landmark.

The [South African Tourism](https://southafrica.net/gl/en/travel) reference supports a national visual language grounded in landscapes, cities, heritage, and diversity. The national hero prompt therefore uses a broad South African landscape with a human-scale path and distant civic/road connection, avoiding a safari montage, flags, or political imagery.

The [UNESCO South Africa state-party page](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/za) confirms the country’s varied heritage and natural contexts, including the Cape Floral Region and iSimangaliso Wetland Park. These references support keeping Cape Town botanically coastal and KwaZulu-Natal warm, green, and subtropical without inventing a meeting venue or using generic resort imagery.

## Visual-search references

The image search pass reviewed broad visual references for South African landscapes, Johannesburg skylines, Cape Town coastal/foothill scenery, and KwaZulu-Natal landscapes. The downloaded search set included a South Africa landscape overview, Johannesburg skyline references, Cape Town coastal references, and KwaZulu-Natal mountain/green landscape references. These were used as directional research only, not as website assets; no third-party image has been copied into the application.

## Prompt acceptance criteria

Each generated asset must be delivered as a standalone WebP at 2400 × 1350 px, 16:9. The left third must remain calm and low-detail for white hero copy; the centre-right must hold the local visual anchor; the focal point must survive a 390–430 px mobile crop; the image must contain no text, logos, watermarks, readable signs, identifiable faces, fabricated meeting venue, drug imagery, staged testimonial, religious symbol, or generic stock-photo treatment. File names, alt text, and page placement are defined in `AREA_SITE_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.
